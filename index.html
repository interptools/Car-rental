Vehicle Details

The rented vehicle is an MG ZS Lux 2025, black, registration plate GBJ 3556. The renter must inspect the vehicle and report any pre-existing damage before departure. The renter must keep the vehicle secure and use it with reasonable care.

Rental Period

The rental begins at the agreed pick-up date and time and ends at the agreed return date and time. The renter must return the vehicle on time to the agreed location. Extensions require the owner's prior approval and may incur additional charges under the separately agreed rate.

Fuel Policy

The vehicle's departure fuel level is recorded on this agreement. Unless the owner agrees otherwise in writing, the renter must return the vehicle with the same fuel level. Refuelling or service charges, if applicable, must be disclosed and agreed by the parties.

Renter's Obligations

The renter confirms that their driver's licence is valid for the rental period and must carry the licence and required vehicle documents while driving. The renter must obey traffic laws, use seat belts, take reasonable precautions against theft, and promptly notify the owner of accidents, breakdowns, damage, or loss. The renter must not allow an unauthorized driver to operate the vehicle.

Damage / Loss

The renter must promptly report any collision, theft, damage, impoundment, or third-party claim and cooperate with lawful reporting and insurance procedures. Responsibility for loss, damage, deductibles, towing, or third-party claims depends on the facts, applicable law, insurance terms, and any separately signed written terms; this template does not create an automatic finding of liability.

Security Deposit

Any security deposit amount, payment method, deductions, and refund timeline must be separately disclosed and agreed in writing before handover. No deposit amount is specified by this form. Deductions should be itemized and supported by reasonable records, subject to applicable law.

Prohibited Use

The vehicle must not be used for unlawful activity, racing, towing without permission, driving while impaired, carrying prohibited goods, sub-renting, or off-road use without the owner's written consent. The renter must not remove, tamper with, or disable safety or tracking equipment.

Return Checklist

At return, the parties should record the return date and time, odometer reading, fuel level, visible condition and any new damage, keys and accessories returned, and any agreed charges. Both parties should retain a copy of the completed condition record.

Privacy and Recordkeeping

The renter understands that the details submitted are used to administer the rental, contact the renter in connection with the rental, document consent, and maintain business records. The owner should restrict access, secure the spreadsheet, retain information only as necessary, and comply with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.
